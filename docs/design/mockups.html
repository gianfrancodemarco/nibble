PRD Mockups

Design mockups v0.2. Picture first, prose last: the agent's explanation becomes an interactive model of your code, served in small nibbles. Sections 02–03 and 05–08 are live, so try them.

Opinions

01Picture first, prose last

Every nibble leads with a visual. The title is one or two sentences, never a paragraph; depth lives in the levelled description.

02Interactive, not animated

Every visual has something to do: scrub, toggle, click, step, compare.

03One canvas per plate

Nibbles move a camera across one shared map. You build a spatial picture, not a slideshow.

04Code is evidence, on demand

Click any node, edge or moment to open the validated lines behind it.

05Small or nothing

≤ 7 nibbles, ≤ 40 words, ≤ 12 nodes. Bigger means chapters.

06No knobs

Agents describe what things are. nibble owns every pixel, so the same idea always looks the same.

03 · The plate (live)

The whole window, not one card

Everything else on this page zooms into one card. The plate is the whole window: its own title and levelled description up top, the keyboard legend on the left — the detail dial lives inside it — and one strip of cards the camera glides across — neighbours stay in sight, so moving is a camera move, not a page swap. Nothing frames the strip from below: held comments float in the corner, and the one send button sits in the title bar. Click a neighbour to move.

05 · Detail dial (live)

Same picture, drawing less

Every plate reads at two depths — overview to decide whether to care, standard to read everything (deep arrives in v0.2). Flip the dial: elements marked standard fade out, but nothing moves — the overview is the same picture drawing less, so what you located at a skim stays exactly where it was. Prose follows the dial too: the description swaps between the skim text and the fuller story. In the reader, d flips it; a selection that isn't drawn at overview falls back to the whole nibble.

06 · Blast radius (live)

The whole PR in three rings

A PR plate opens with a ringed map, read inside-out: what changed, the dependents that didn't change but rely on it, and the rest for orientation. Every dependent you haven't looked at carries a badge — click the three badged files and the counter ticks down to done. The count lives in this tab only: nothing about what you did or didn't open is ever sent.

07 · Not-shown ledger (live)

What the walkthrough never showed

A diff plate ends with one more card: every changed file of the diff as a tree, computed by nibble from git — never by the agent — so the walkthrough cannot quietly skip anything. Covered files carry a shown badge; the rest spell out their unshown line ranges. Click a file to read its hunks in the drawer — the never-shown lines highlighted, gaps elided, removed code as it was on the base side — and comment on it like anything else: the target reaches the agent as ledger:path.

11 · Agent side

Strict in, structured out

The CLI enforces the opinions with fix-it messages. The blocked nibble serve exits with the review.

✗ rejected: nibble has no visual. Add --visual (map|sequence|timeline|compare).
