Answering

Comments are pinned to the thing itself: select a node, an edge, a moment on the timeline or some lines in the drawer, and press c.

Two ways to send a comment:

Ask now

Ends the agent's wait immediately with your question. The answer comes back threaded into the same plate.

Hold for review

Keeps the comment until you send your review with a verdict: approve, changes, question or close.

Choosing a verdict does not send anything. Clicking approve (or any other verdict) only selects it. The plate stays open, the agent keeps waiting, and you can change your mind or add comments. The review is posted only when you click send review. That click is what ends the agent’s wait, and the verdict becomes its exit code. No stray click can close a review.

In a PR review, check the end of the plate before approving. The not-shown ledger lists every hunk of the diff no nibble referenced, and the map badges dependents the plate never visited (callers, tests, configs of the changed code). That's where agent-written PRs usually go wrong. Both are open to comments like anything else.

Comments travel back pinned to what you selected, each carrying its trust level, so the agent knows exactly what you meant even if the code moved.
