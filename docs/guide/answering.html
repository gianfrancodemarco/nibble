Answering

Disagree on the thing itself: select a node, an edge, a moment on the timeline or lines in the drawer, and press c.

Two ways to send a comment:

Ask now

Ends the agent's wait immediately with your question. The answer comes back threaded into the same plate.

Hold for review

Keeps the comment until you send your review with a verdict: approve, changes, question or close.

Choosing a verdict is a draft, sending is the commitment. Clicking approve (or any verdict) only selects it — the plate stays open, the agent keeps waiting, you can change your mind or add comments. One explicit send review click posts it; that single deliberate act is what ends the agent’s wait, as its exit code. No stray click ever closes a review.

In a PR review, check the end of the plate before approving: the not-shown ledger lists every hunk of the diff no nibble referenced, and the map badges dependents the plate never visited — callers, tests, configs of changed code. That's where agent-written PRs go wrong, and both are open to comments like anything else.

Comments travel back pinned to what you selected, each carrying its trust level, so the agent knows exactly what you meant even if the code moved.
